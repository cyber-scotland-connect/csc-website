Cyber Scotland Aberdeen, hosted by TechForce Cyber in partnership with Robert Gordon University and Arctic Wolf during CyberScotland Week.

Focuses on real-world threat landscapes for Scottish SMBs and enterprises, incident response case studies, evaluating internal vs managed Security Operations Centres (SOCs), and lessons learned from major Scottish public breaches.

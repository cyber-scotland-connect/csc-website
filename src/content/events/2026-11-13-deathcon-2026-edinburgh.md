DEATHCon (Detection Engineering and Threat Hunting Conference) is coming to Edinburgh with an official in-person satellite gathering at the Royal Botanic Garden on 13–14 November 2026, running alongside its globally-distributed event.

DEATHCon is a 100% hands-on technical conference focused on detection engineering, defensive tradecraft, and threat hunting through interactive workshops, live hacking laboratories, and CTF challenges. The Edinburgh physical hub allows Scottish and UK practitioners to collaborate in person, tackle lab scenarios together, and connect over catered lunches and social networking.

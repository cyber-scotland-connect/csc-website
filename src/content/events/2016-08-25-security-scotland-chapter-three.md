> **Historical Note:** This gathering was hosted under our founding community moniker, **Security Scotland**, prior to our official merger and rebrand to **Cyber Scotland Connect** announced at DIGIT Expo in November 2018.

Security Scotland Chapter 3 brought together 60 infosec professionals and students at Skyscanner's Edinburgh office for two guest presentations and community discussion.

> **Milestone Event:** The official founding announcement of Cyber Scotland Connect!

Harry McLaren and Stu Hirst hosted a keynote panel session during DIGIT Expo at the Edinburgh International Conference Centre (EICC). With 74 attendees in the session, they officially announced the merger of regional Scottish infosec meetups under a single unified banner: **Cyber Scotland Connect (CSC)**.

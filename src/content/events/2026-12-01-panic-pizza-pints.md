Hosted by [DECID:R](https://decidr.live/) and [Vault 8 Labs](https://vault8labs.co.uk/), **Panic, Pizza & Pints** is an informal Edinburgh meetup combining an interactive cyber crisis simulation with free pizza, drinks, and community networking.

Attendees are placed in the hot seat to navigate a series of crisis decisions and see how the consequences unfold—with no technical expertise required, no sales presentations, and no death by PowerPoint.

### What's Included

- Free entry (spaces limited to 30 attendees)
- Pizza and drinks, courtesy of the event sponsors
- Interactive cyber crisis simulation
- Networking with Edinburgh's cyber, IT, risk, and compliance community

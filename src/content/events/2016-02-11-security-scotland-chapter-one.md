> **Historical Note:** This gathering was hosted under our founding community moniker, **Security Scotland**, prior to our official merger and rebrand to **Cyber Scotland Connect** announced at DIGIT Expo in November 2018.

The very first Security Scotland meetup, hosted by Skyscanner at their Quartermile One headquarters in Edinburgh. Attended by 54 pioneering practitioners, setting the foundation for open, accessible, vendor-pitch-free infosec community in Scotland.

The 10th annual **Strathclyde International Perspectives on Cybercrime Summer School** takes place from 23–27 August 2027 at the University of Strathclyde in Glasgow. Jointly organised with Simon Fraser University (Canada), OTH Amberg-Weiden, OTH Regensburg, and HAW Kiel (Germany), the week-long programme opens with a one-day research symposium on Monday 23 August followed by four days of interdisciplinary lectures, interactive workshops, and panel discussions for students, researchers, industry practitioners, and law enforcement.

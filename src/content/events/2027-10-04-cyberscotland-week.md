CyberScotland Week 2027 takes place from 4–10 October 2027 across Scotland, moving to October to align with International Cyber Security Month.

The national flagship week brings together businesses, public sector bodies, educational institutions, and community groups to promote cyber awareness, foster collaboration, and build cyber resilience across Scotland.

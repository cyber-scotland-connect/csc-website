In partnership with Royal Bank of Scotland and Fortinet, Cyber and Fraud Centre Scotland hosts the **See It, Be It – Women and Girls in Cyber Conference 2026** at RBS Gogarburn, Edinburgh.

The free conference brings together female high school pupils, college and university students, educators, cyber professionals, and industry leaders for panel discussions with diverse role models, hands-on activities, and small-group mentoring sessions.

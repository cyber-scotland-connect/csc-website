> **Historical Note:** This gathering was hosted under our founding community moniker, **Security Scotland**, prior to our official merger and rebrand to **Cyber Scotland Connect** announced at DIGIT Expo in November 2018.

An extraordinary deep-dive into historical cryptography: 41 attendees joined us at Skyscanner's Edinburgh office to examine an authentic World War II Enigma Machine up close and learn about historical cyphers and cryptanalysis.

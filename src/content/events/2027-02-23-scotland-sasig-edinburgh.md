The SASIG (Security Awareness Special Interest Group) Scotland regional session in Edinburgh. Free to attend for cybersecurity professionals, public sector defenders, and academics under the Chatham House Rule.

Features collaborative panels on national cyber resilience, threat landscape evolution, leadership readiness under pressure, and networking. Delegates earn CPD/CPE points for ISC2, ISACA, and Security Institute accreditations.
